# Co-driving research assets

Source: Peng Lu's supplied local Notion export, `co-driving/Strategic Research & Framework Development Designi/`.

These are the original research diagrams, instruments, illustrations, field recruitment photograph, and toolkit-in-use material. PNG assets are losslessly re-encoded at their original pixel dimensions to reduce transfer size; no visual content is altered. Stock photographs and decorative social/contact images from the export were not included. The earlier asset set is preserved for compatibility.

The case study follows the exported narrative but distinguishes research interpretation, conceptual models, and practitioner feedback from measured product impact. Interview rounds are reported as 12 and 10 participants rather than repeating the export's inconsistent “over 22” summary. The experience-pattern matrix uses the export's participant codes; it is not a population-level statistical result.
