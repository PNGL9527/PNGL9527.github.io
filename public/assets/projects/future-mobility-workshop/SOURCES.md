## Evidence boundaries

- The teaching period is 2022–2023. 28 students and six teams are the documented 2022 cohort, not a total for two rounds. Sustainability sections 4–5 document 21 September–30 October 2022, three parallel strands, paired near/far groups, all 28 questionnaire responses. Applied Sciences describes six weeks and five follow-up interviewees.
- Publications are overlapping accounts, not two independent cohorts to sum. Applied Sciences' wording about 28 participants and two station groups is not used to claim 28 students in the station strand alone.
- Near/far horizons are relative to the workshop. Sustainability uses +5 / +50 years; Applied Sciences uses 5–10 / 50 years for station cases. They are not contemporary predictions or evidence of actual mobility technology capability.
- Course English title varies: supplied portfolio says Principles and Practices of Product Design; papers use Product Design Concept and Practice / Product Design Theory and Practice. Page uses the neutral final-year Product Design course rather than assert one official English title.
- TA contribution and proposed teaching improvements come from the supplied portfolio, not inferred solely from academic author lists. The workshop framework is not attributed to Peng Lu alone.
- Selected student works demonstrate outputs, not controlled evidence of learning gains. Surveys and interviews are participant reflections. No efficacy, real-world deployment, accessibility validation or 2023 survey count is invented.
- Teaching research contributions are sourced to the two CRediT statements; academic supervision by senior coauthors is not confused with student-project tutorials. Individual student names and exclusive ownership of assets are not inferred.
- No original source PDFs, private contact details or participant questionnaire records were uploaded to public assets.
