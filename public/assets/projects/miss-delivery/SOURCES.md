## Evidence and adaptation decisions

- Brief and context: report PDF pp4–14 (printed pp1–11). This is an academic proposal concerning STEF, not a claimed commissioned or launched engagement.
- Personas and journey: report PDF pp15–17 (printed pp12–14). They are constructed scenarios, not documented research participants. Persona details condensed into a comparison table.
- Workflow and operations: report PDF pp18–26 (printed pp15–23). Pre-login tracking, helper time windows, capacity matching, packaging standardisation, fifteen-minute request response and double routing are proposed, not implemented.
- Trust: use the final report's multidimensional and mutually withheld feedback, not just the presentation's early single-star mockup.
- Business model: report PDF pp27–30 (printed pp24–27). Initial investment €100,350 and proposed 10% commission from year three are historical assumptions. No NPV/ROI headline is reproduced; underlying forecasts have not been independently validated, and tables differ in some advertising/cash-flow assumptions.
- Pricing varies between review mockups and final report. Preserve the originals visually but do not promote their numbers as definitive live pricing.
- Team and personal contribution: report PDF pp32–37 (printed pp29–34); p36 / printed p33 explicitly identifies Peng Lu as team leader and describes his design synthesis connecting the group's perspectives. Do not assign exclusive authorship of the app, research or financial model.
- Food safety, accountability and disputes are described as requirements before a future pilot, not as implemented controls or original validated outcomes.
- All six team members credited; professor and tutor from report PDF p2. Publication date is website-entry date; `year` is the author's actual project year.
- No source PDFs or standalone persona photographs were copied into the public asset folder. Portraits embedded within the original journey map remain part of that artwork.
