## The Problem Context

The project's vision was to promote Uniqueness and Self-Acceptance (Vision: A Story about Uniqueness) by enabling teenagers to feel more comfortable with the physiological changes they experience.

To solve this, we moved beyond just a product and defined a customized service strategy to help teenagers:
1. Understand and love their bodies better.
2. Focus on self-improvement through physical activity and regular feedback.

---

### System Overview: Bridging Biometrics and Emotional Well-being

To deliver on this principle, a.wear was conceived as a **Product-Service System (PSS)** that translates physical change into actionable, positive digital feedback.

## Why an App?

- **Continuous interaction:** The problem unfolds across the day, not at a single moment  
- **Personalized feedback loops:** Data interpretation and guidance require contextual adaptation  
- **Coordination hub:** The app mediates between wearable sensing, user reflection, and action  

One strong paragraph or three bullets is enough.

> This makes the app a **design necessity**, not an arbitrary choice.

---

## The App Concept

Now you introduce the app **as a response**, not as a starting point.

- What role does the app play?
- What kind of relationship does it establish with the user?
- What is it *not* trying to be?

## Key Flows

A prototype-first page should expose 3–5 “task stories” that a reviewer can scan fast.

1. **Onboarding → device pairing → personalization**
2. **Daily check-in → recommendation → action**
3. **Real-time feedback → alert → resolution**
4. **History → insights → behavior loop**
5. **Settings → privacy / data control**

For each flow, include 1–2 supporting screens (or one mini storyboard).

## Interaction Model

这一段是你要“强调交互原型”的核心：不是讲过程，而是讲你做出的交互决策。

### Navigation & IA

- Primary navigation: tabs / bottom nav / progressive disclosure
- Key objects: device, plan, check-in, insights, alerts

### Feedback & States

- Loading / empty / error states
- Success confirmations
- Critical alerts escalation

### Microinteractions (Optional)

- Haptics, motion, progressive feedback, gestures

## Design Rationale (Short)

只写“为什么这样设计”，不要写“我做了哪些步骤”。

- Why the loop is structured this way …
- Why feedback is shown as …
- Why notifications are …

## System Context (Minimal)

用最小篇幅交代这个 app 在服务系统中的位置（如果你想保留 service design 的底色）。

- Actors: user / wearable / service provider
- Data flow: what is sensed → what is computed → what is shown
- Constraints: privacy, battery, compliance, etc.
