## Overview

BookHero is a strategic UX redesign project aiming to improve library resource utilization and support user co-construction through participatory digital experiences.

<img
  src="/assets/projects/bookhero/cover.jpg"
  alt="BookHero cover"
/>

---

## The Problem

Despite rich collections, many library resources remain underutilized.
Users often struggle with discovery, relevance, and meaningful engagement within existing digital library systems.

---

## Design Goal

- Improve discoverability of library resources  
- Encourage user participation and co-construction  
- Reduce friction in searching and organizing content  

---

## The Solution

BookHero introduces a redesigned UX framework that rethinks search, exploration, and participatory features, enabling users to actively curate, share, and co-construct library content.

---

## Key Outcomes

- Redesigned information architecture for search and discovery  
- New interaction flows supporting user-created collections  
- High-fidelity prototype validated through user feedback  

---

## Design Process (Brief)

1. Research & discovery  
2. User interviews and insight synthesis  
3. Ideation and concept refinement  
4. Prototyping and usability validation
