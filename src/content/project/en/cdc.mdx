## The Project Context

This project explores the renewal of the Milan Chamber of Commerce (CdC) through a Product–Service System design approach. It proposes **Salone del Futuro** as a service–space concept that places training and knowledge dissemination at the core, supporting micro and small businesses in responding to technological, regulatory, and market changes.

## Design Rationale

- **Data-to-Action Feedback Loop:** Eliminating Friction
- **Gamification & Novel Interaction:** Expressive & Playful Visual Language
- **Sensitive Data Configuration:** Designing with Empathy

---

## Outcome

- Successfully translated a sensitive emotional issue (teen body image pressure) into an empathetic, actionable app experience.
- Dual-mode feedback (immediate alerts vs. casual cloud prompts) balanced guidance, autonomy, and non-judgmental interaction.
- Expressive, gamified UI elements proved effective in building trust, engagement, and a youth-oriented brand identity.

---

## Reflection
- Designing for health-sensitive contexts requires low-friction, conversational feedback rather than metric-heavy, didactic UI.
- Integrating app UX with physical hardware highlighted the importance of simple, on-demand control over complex configurations.
- Limited time restricted iterative testing with teenage users, especially for fine-tuning emotional tone and alert phrasing.
