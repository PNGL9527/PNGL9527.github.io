## The Project Context

This project explores the renewal of the Milan Chamber of Commerce (CdC) through a Product–Service System design approach. It proposes **Salone del Futuro** as a service–space concept that places training and knowledge dissemination at the core, supporting micro and small businesses in responding to technological, regulatory, and market changes.

## Design Concept

Based on the interview, we define the target group as micro and small companies, which have less than 50 employees. Since they are too small, sometimes they are not able to deal with some documentary formalities.

### Practical Training as the Core of the Service

<figure class="m-0">
  <LightboxImage
    src={`${import.meta.env.BASE_URL}assets/projects/cdc/cdc_ways.jpg`}
    alt="CdC's New Roles."
    className="rounded-2xl w-full block"
  />
</figure>

The program aims to develop more resilient small and micro enterprises, offering help by Disseminating Knowledge:

- Innovations in technology, production, methods, organisations.
- New laws and regulations that may (or will) affect companies.
- Incoming trends for the current market.
- Expansion to new markets for a certain sector.

#### Self-Driving Tractors as an Example

Assuming there is a new technology trend of *Self-Driving Tractors* in agriculture, and after investigation, the new CdC group takes it as an important opportunity for related companies.

<figure class="m-0">
  <LightboxImage
    src={`${import.meta.env.BASE_URL}assets/projects/cdc/cdc_example.jpg`}
    alt="The creation part of the proposed new roles of the CdC"
    className="rounded-2xl w-full block"
  />
</figure>

The Surveying and Forecasting Lab inside CdC would connect tutors, consultancy firms, and other partners in the agriculture domain. They would co-design the new training course together. As a result, the new training course could be implemented for S&M companies. During the training, some certain tools could also be used to amplify the efficacy.

### Training Service: The Toolkit and the App

In our design concept, CdC also offers the training service for small businesses, facilitated by a set of tools and the App.

#### The Toolkit

<figure class="m-0">
  <LightboxImage
    src={`${import.meta.env.BASE_URL}assets/projects/cdc/cdc_kits-4.jpg`}
    alt="The service kits part of the proposed new roles of the CdC"
    className="rounded-2xl w-full block"
  />
  <figcaption class="mt-2 text-sm leading-snug text-neutral-500  text-center">
    The service toolkit facilitating the Service role of the CdC.
  </figcaption>
</figure>

With the service toolkit, CdC can offer training for small businesses with 4 directions:
1. **Open your mind:** training based on *the Challenge Canvas Guide.*
2. **Open your eyes:** training based on ***Case study (PEST and SWOT)*** and ***Scorecard System.***
3. **Open to innovation:** training based on *New Challenge Canvas*, *Risk Management*, and ***Strategic Action Plan***.
4. **Open to society:** training based on *Idea List* tool.
